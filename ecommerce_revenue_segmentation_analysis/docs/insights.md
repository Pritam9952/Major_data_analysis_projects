### Ecommerce Revenue Segmentation Analysis

## 🛒 1. Top Category

* **Top Category:** BEAUTY (₹5.55M revenue)

💡 Insight:

* All categories contribute almost equally (~16–17%)

## 🌍 2. Region Performance

* **Top Region:** MIDDLE EAST (₹8.30M)
* **Lowest Region:** EUROPE (₹8.11M)

💡 Insight:

* Shows balanced performance across all regions
* No significant weak region

---

## 💸 3. Discount Impact

| Discount | Revenue    |
| -------- | ---------- |
| 0–5%     | Highest    |
| 10–30%   | Decreasing |

💡 Insight:

* Shows low discount gives highest revenue
* High discount reduces revenue

---

## 📅 4. Peak Months

* **Top Months:** Jan, Jul, May

💡 Insight:

* Shows seasonal trend in revenue
* Certain months drive higher sales

---

## 💳 5. Payment Method

* **Top Method:** WALLET

💡 Insight:

* Shows digital payments slightly higher than others

---

## ⭐ 6. Rating Impact

💡 Insight:

* Shows no strong relation between rating and revenue

---

## 📦 7. Category Contribution

* Each category contributes ~16–17%

💡 Insight:

* Shows no dominant category
* Revenue is evenly distributed

---

## 🧠 Final Summary

* Shows business is balanced across categories and regions
* Shows no major dependency on any single segment
* Shows low discount strategy performs better
* Shows seasonal sales pattern present

# 🚀 Recommendations

* Focus on top category (BEAUTY)
