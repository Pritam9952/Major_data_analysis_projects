# 🚀 Business Recommendations

### Ecommerce Revenue Optimization Strategy

---

## 🛒 1. Focus on High Revenue Categories

* Promote **BEAUTY category** through:

  * Ads
  * Bundles
  * Featured listings

### 🎯 Action

* Increase visibility of top-performing categories

---

## 🌍 2. Improve Weak Regions

* Europe shows slightly lower performance

### 🎯 Action

* Region-specific campaigns
* Localized offers
* Improve logistics or delivery experience

---

## 💸 3. Optimize Discount Strategy

* High discounts reduce revenue

### 🎯 Action

* Focus on **low discount (0–5%) strategy**
* Avoid unnecessary heavy discounting

---

## 📅 4. Leverage Peak Months

* High sales in Jan, May, July

### 🎯 Action

* Launch campaigns before peak months
* Increase inventory & marketing spend

---

## 💳 5. Promote Digital Payments

* Wallet & UPI performing best

### 🎯 Action

* Offer cashback on Wallet/UPI
* Encourage digital transactions

---

## ⭐ 6. Improve Product Ratings

* Ratings not strongly impacting revenue
* But improving them increases trust

### 🎯 Action

* Improve product quality
* Encourage customer reviews

---

## 🎯 Final Strategy

* Maintain balanced portfolio
* Focus on optimization (not expansion)
* Improve retention and efficiency

---
