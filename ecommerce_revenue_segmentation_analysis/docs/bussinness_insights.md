# 🧠 RFM Analysis

| Region      | Recency  | Frequency | Monetary | Segment         |
| ----------- | -------- | --------- | -------- | --------------- |
| All Regions | 823 days | High      | High     | Loyal Customers |

### 💡 Insight

* Customers are **loyal but not recent**
* Indicates potential **customer inactivity risk**

---

# 🚀 Final Business Insights

* Business has a **strong and balanced customer base**
* All regions behave as **VIP customers**
* No weak segment → low risk in revenue distribution
* However:

  * High recency (823 days) = **lack of recent engagement**

---

# 📌 Business Recommendations

* Launch **re-engagement campaigns**
* Offer **discounts or promotions** to inactive customers
* Focus on **retention strategies**
* Maintain loyalty through personalized marketing
