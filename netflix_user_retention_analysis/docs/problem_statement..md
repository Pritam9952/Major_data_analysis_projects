# 📊 Netflix Customer Churn Analysis

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Customer churn is a critical challenge for subscription-based businesses like Netflix, as it directly impacts revenue and long-term growth. High churn rates indicate that customers are leaving the platform, which increases customer acquisition costs and reduces overall profitability.

The objective of this project is to analyze customer data to identify key factors contributing to churn and uncover patterns in user behavior. By leveraging SQL, Python, and Power BI, the project aims to generate actionable insights that can help improve customer retention strategies.

---

## 🚀 Objectives

* Analyze customer data to identify churn patterns
* Calculate key business metrics such as churn rate, retention rate, and revenue
* Segment customers based on demographics, services, and usage behavior
* Identify high-risk customer groups likely to churn
* Provide data-driven recommendations to reduce churn and improve retention

---

## 🧠 Business Context

In highly competitive streaming platforms, retaining existing customers is more cost-effective than acquiring new ones. Understanding why customers leave and addressing those issues can significantly improve customer lifetime value (CLTV) and overall business performance.

This project focuses on translating raw customer data into meaningful insights that support strategic decision-making and enhance customer experience.
