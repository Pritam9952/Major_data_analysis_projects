# 📊 Key Insights

This document highlights the major findings derived from customer churn analysis using SQL, Python, and Power BI.

---

## 🔥 1. Contract Type Drives Churn

Customers with **month-to-month contracts show the highest churn (~40%+)**, compared to long-term contracts.

- Short-term contracts offer flexibility, making it easier for customers to leave
- One-year and two-year contracts significantly improve retention

👉 **Insight:** Contract duration is one of the strongest predictors of churn  
👉 **Business Impact:** Encouraging long-term subscriptions can reduce churn significantly

---

## ⏳ 2. New Customers Are High-Risk

Customers in the **first year (0–12 months)** have the highest churn rates.

- Churn decreases as customer tenure increases
- Long-term customers show higher loyalty and stability

👉 **Insight:** Early-stage customer lifecycle is critical  
👉 **Business Impact:** Improving onboarding and early engagement can reduce churn

---

## 💳 3. Payment Method Influences Retention

Customers using **electronic check payment methods have higher churn rates (~35%+)**

- Automatic payment methods (credit card/bank transfer) show better retention
- Manual payments indicate lower engagement

👉 **Insight:** Payment behavior reflects customer commitment  
👉 **Business Impact:** Promoting auto-pay can improve retention

---

## 🌐 4. Internet Service Type Matters

Customers using **fiber optic internet show higher churn (~30%+)**

- Likely due to higher pricing or service expectations
- DSL and no-internet users show relatively lower churn

👉 **Insight:** Premium users are more sensitive to service quality  
👉 **Business Impact:** Improving service reliability can reduce churn in high-value segments

---

## 💰 5. High Monthly Charges Increase Churn

Customers with **higher monthly charges show increased churn probability**

- Price sensitivity plays a major role in churn behavior
- High-paying users expect better value and service quality

👉 **Insight:** Pricing directly impacts retention  
👉 **Business Impact:** Optimizing pricing and offering value-based plans can reduce churn

---

## 📦 6. Service Bundling Improves Retention

Customers with **multiple subscribed services are less likely to churn**

- Bundled services increase engagement and dependency
- Single-service users show higher churn rates

👉 **Insight:** Engagement reduces churn  
👉 **Business Impact:** Offering bundled services can increase customer stickiness

---

## 👴 7. Senior Citizens Show Slightly Higher Churn

Senior citizens show **slightly higher churn rates (~40% vs ~24%)**

- May face usability challenges or pricing sensitivity
- Require better support and simplified experience

👉 **Insight:** Demographic factors influence churn  
👉 **Business Impact:** Personalized support can improve retention

---

## 🎯 Overall Summary

Customer churn is driven by a combination of:

- Contract type  
- Tenure (customer lifecycle stage)  
- Pricing (monthly charges)  
- Service usage  
- Payment behavior  

---

## 💀 Final Takeaway

👉 High churn risk =  
**Short-term contract + Low tenure + High charges + Low service usage**

👉 Low churn risk =  
**Long-term contract + High tenure + Multiple services**

---

## 🚀 Business Conclusion

Understanding these patterns enables companies to:

- Improve customer retention strategies  
- Increase customer lifetime value (CLTV)  
- Reduce revenue loss due to churn  

👉 Data-driven insights help transform customer data into actionable business decisions.
